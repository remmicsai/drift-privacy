Privacy Policy

Drift In (“Drift,” “the app,” “we,” “us”)  ·  Effective date: November 10, 2026

Drift In is a sleep and wellness journaling app. This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. In short: Drift does not require an account, and the things you write stay on your device.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect your personal information on our servers. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location, and we do not run user accounts.

2. Information stored on your device

The following is saved locally on your phone and is not sent to us:

  • Journal entries (“bottles”) you write in the app.
  • App preferences and progress, such as whether premium is unlocked and your daily reading count.
  • Reminder settings, such as the day and time you choose for bedtime reminders.

Because this data lives only on your device, it is removed when you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app. We cannot recover it for you.

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders locally on your device. No notification content or schedule is sent to us. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

4. Purchases

Drift offers a one-time in-app purchase to unlock premium content. Payments are processed by Google Play; we never see or store your payment card details. To verify your purchase and let you restore it, we use RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat may receive an anonymous app user identifier and purchase information (for example, which product you bought and when). See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. Audio

The app plays ocean sounds and other audio that is bundled with the app. It does not use your microphone.

6. Advertising and analytics

Drift does not currently show ads, and we do not use advertising IDs or third-party analytics or tracking tools. If this changes, we will update this policy before the change takes effect.

7. Support and crisis resources

The “Support & Hotlines” screen lists phone numbers, text lines, and websites of independent organizations. When you tap a call, text, or website button, your phone opens the relevant app or browser. Those contacts are handled by the organizations themselves, under their own privacy practices. We are not affiliated with them, and we do not see or record whom you contact.

Drift is not a medical service and does not provide therapy, diagnosis, or emergency care. If you are in immediate danger, call 911 (in the US). If you are thinking about suicide, call or text 988.

8. Sharing of information

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties except the purchase-processing providers described in Section 4, as needed to complete and verify your purchase, or if required by law.

9. Data retention and deletion

We do not hold your journal data, so there is nothing for us to delete on our end. To delete everything the app has stored, clear the app’s storage in your device settings or uninstall the app. For questions about purchase records held by Google or RevenueCat, contact those providers or us at the address below.

10. Children’s privacy

Drift is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help resolve it.

11. Security

Your data stays on your device, so its security depends on your device’s protections (screen lock, encryption, backups). We encourage you to use a device passcode.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will be reflected in the app or on this page.
